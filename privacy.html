Privacy Policy

This page explains how information is used to operate and secure the MDH platform.

1. Information we collect

MDH may process registration details such as your name, phone number, email address, referral information, account status, and information you submit for deposits, withdrawals, KYC, support, or other platform features.

2. How information is used

Information is used to authenticate accounts, provide platform services, process and review financial requests, maintain referrals and machine records, send notifications, provide support, prevent abuse, and protect the platform.

3. Financial information

Transaction information submitted for Mobile Money deposits or withdrawals is used to verify and process the relevant request. Users should never submit passwords, authentication codes, or other unrelated secrets in transaction notes.

4. Storage and security

MDH uses Supabase services for authentication, database operations, and storage where configured. Access should be restricted through authentication, authorization, and database security policies. No frontend code should contain private service credentials.

5. Notifications and support

We may use your account information to send service notifications about deposits, withdrawals, machines, KYC, referrals, security, and important platform announcements. Support conversations may be processed to answer your request.

6. Mara AI

Mara AI is an automated support assistant. It should use only information authorized for the current user and should not expose private information, invent transaction details, or bypass account restrictions.

7. Your responsibilities

Keep your password and authentication information private. Report suspicious activity promptly and use accurate information when submitting account or transaction requests.

8. Policy updates

This policy may be updated as the platform, security requirements, or applicable obligations change. Updated versions will be published on this page.

9. Questions

For privacy or account questions, use the official support channels available through MDH. Do not send passwords or other sensitive authentication secrets to support.
