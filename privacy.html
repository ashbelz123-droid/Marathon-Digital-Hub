Privacy Policy

How MDH handles information needed to operate, secure and improve the platform.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the services you use, MDH may process your name, phone number, email, profile information, referral activity, wallet information, machine activity, notifications and transaction records.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used for authentication, account management, requested platform services, transaction processing, security, notifications, support and platform improvement.

3. Payments & Transactions

Payment and withdrawal information may be processed to identify requests, maintain records and support transaction review. Never include passwords or authentication codes in transaction notes.

4. Supabase & Storage

Where configured, MDH uses Supabase for authentication, database operations and storage. Access should be protected through authentication, authorization and appropriate database security policies.

5. Mara AI

Mara AI is an automated support feature. It should only access information authorized for the current account and should never expose private information or bypass account restrictions.

6. Security

Users must keep passwords and verification codes private. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so secure your device and report suspicious activity promptly.

7. Information Sharing

Information may be shared when necessary to provide requested services, operate the platform, maintain security or meet applicable legal obligations.

8. Your Responsibilities

  • Keep credentials private.
  • Use accurate account information.
  • Secure your device.
  • Use official support channels for account concerns.

9. Updates

This policy may change as MDH features, security practices or legal requirements evolve. The latest version published on this page applies to continued use of the platform.
