Payment Instructions

🟡 MTN Mobile Money

  1. Dial *165#.
  2. Select Send Money.
  3. Enter the MTN number above.
  4. Enter the amount you want to deposit.
  5. Confirm the payment using your PIN.
  6. Wait for the MTN confirmation SMS.
  7. Copy the Transaction ID from the SMS.
  8. Return here and submit your deposit request.

🔴 Airtel Money

  1. Dial *185#.
  2. Select Send Money.
  3. Enter the Airtel number above.
  4. Enter the amount you want to deposit.
  5. Confirm the payment using your PIN.
  6. Wait for the Airtel confirmation SMS.
  7. Copy the Transaction ID from the SMS.
  8. Return here and submit your deposit request.
⚠️ Deposit requests are verified manually. Approval time depends on verification.
