Payment Instructions

🟡 MTN Mobile Money

  1. Dial *165#.
  2. Select Send Money.
  3. Enter the MTN number shown above.
  4. Enter the deposit amount.
  5. Confirm using your PIN.
  6. Wait for the confirmation SMS.
  7. Copy the Transaction ID from the SMS.

🔴 Airtel Money

  1. Dial *185#.
  2. Select Send Money.
  3. Enter the Airtel number shown above.
  4. Enter the deposit amount.
  5. Confirm using your PIN.
  6. Wait for the confirmation SMS.
  7. Copy the Transaction ID from the SMS.
