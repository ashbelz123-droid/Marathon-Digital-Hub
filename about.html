About MDH

A mobile-first digital platform designed to bring account management, mining activity, wallet information, referrals and crypto-market information together in one clear experience.

Who We Are

Marathon Digital Hub is a digital technology platform focused on mining technology, digital innovation and online platform services. Our goal is to make the member experience simple, clear and accessible from a mobile device.

Our Mission

To provide accessible digital opportunities through modern technology while continuously improving usability, security, transparency and platform services.

Our Vision

To grow into a trusted digital innovation platform serving users across Africa and beyond through practical technology and responsible platform development.

Our Core Values

What You Can Do With MDH

Members can access their dashboard, view mining machines, monitor account activity, manage referrals, review available crypto information, receive notifications and use Mara AI for platform guidance.

Security & Responsible Use

Members should protect their passwords and verification codes. Account information should come from connected platform data, and users should review the applicable terms before using platform services.
