Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about accounts, machines, wallet activity, referrals, withdrawals and platform security.

How do I register?
Open the Register page, enter the required information and complete account creation. Use accurate information so your account can be managed correctly.
How do I make a deposit?
Open the Deposit page and follow the currently available payment instructions. Always confirm the destination and amount before sending funds.
How do I activate a machine?
After your wallet has sufficient funds, open Machines, review the available machine information and follow the purchase or activation process shown by the platform.
How does the referral system work?
Open Referral Centre to find your personal referral code and link. Share the link with people who want to join. Eligible referral activity and rewards are displayed from connected account data.
How do I withdraw?
Open the Withdraw page, enter the requested amount and payment details, then submit the request. Processing may require platform review before completion.
How long do withdrawals take?
Processing time can depend on account review, request status and the payment method used. Check your transaction status for the latest information.
I forgot my password. What should I do?
Use the Forgot Password option on the Login page and follow the password-reset instructions sent to your registered email. Never share reset links or verification codes.
Are my balances and statistics real?
The application is designed to display connected account data. If a value is unavailable, it should show an appropriate empty state rather than inventing a balance or activity figure.
Is my account secure?
Use a strong unique password, protect your email and verification codes, and never give another person access to your account. MDH staff should not need your password.
How can I get help?
Use the available Support page or open Mara AI for platform guidance. For account-specific issues, provide only the information required to identify the issue.
