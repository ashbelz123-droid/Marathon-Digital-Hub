Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register?

Click the Register button, fill in your details and create your account.

How do I make a deposit?

Open the Deposit page, select your payment method and follow the instructions.

How do I activate a machine?

After funding your wallet, visit the Machines page and purchase a machine.

How do I withdraw?

Go to Withdraw, enter your amount and mobile money number, then submit your request.

How does the referral system work?

Share your referral link with friends. When they register and become active, you may earn referral rewards according to the platform rules.

I forgot my password. What should I do?

Contact customer support for assistance with account recovery.

How long do withdrawals take?

Withdrawal processing times depend on platform review and payment provider processing.

How can I contact support?

Visit the Support page to contact customer care through WhatsApp, email, or other available channels.

Is my account secure?

Use a strong password and never share your login details with anyone.
